Cookie Policy

Which cookies and similar technologies swkpropertiesuae.com uses, and how to control them.

Photo: Hongbin / Unsplash

Last updated 26 October 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use similar browser storage to remember choices you make on the site.

Essential

Needed for the site to work. We store your cookie choice, whether you dismissed the announcement bar, and whether you have already seen our newsletter pop-up. These do not identify you and cannot be switched off.

Analytics

Only if you allow it. Analytics tools such as Google Analytics tell us which pages are visited so we can improve them. IP addresses are anonymised.

Marketing

Only if you allow it. These measure how well our advertising performs. We do not sell your data.

Your choices

Use the Cookie settings link in the footer to change your choice at any time. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about cookies: info@swkpropertiesuae.com.
