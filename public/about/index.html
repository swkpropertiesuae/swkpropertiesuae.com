Ten years of getting Dubai property right

SWK Properties was founded on a simple idea: buying a home in Dubai — whether you live down the road or on the other side of the world — should feel transparent, not transactional.

Photo: Dynamic Hong Kong / Unsplash
Our Story

Built by agents, run for clients

SWK Properties was started in 2016 by a small team of agents frustrated with how opaque property buying in the UAE could feel for outsiders. Today we're a full-service brokerage of over 40 consultants, but the founding principle hasn't changed: every fee, every payment plan clause, and every ROI figure gets explained in plain language before you sign anything.

We work across off-plan and secondary sales, mortgage arrangement, and long-term property management — so the relationship doesn't end at handover.

Our People

Meet a few of our consultants

Every client is paired with a dedicated consultant for the life of their purchase.

Mohammad Haseeb

Sales & Operations Manager

Mohammad Haseeb brings sales and day-to-day operations together, keeping the property search, paperwork and handover moving in the same direction. His focus is straightforward: a clear next step for the client and a team that follows through.

Mohd Shahid Khan

Office Admin & IT Executive, India

Ensures smooth day-to-day office operations while managing the company's IT systems and digital infrastructure. Coordinates administrative processes, streamlines workflows, and provides reliable technical support to keep the business running efficiently.

Issaadi Chahrazed

Off-Plan Property Consultant, Algeria

Specializes in Dubai's off-plan properties, helping clients identify high-potential investment opportunities. Provides expert advice on new launches, payment plans, and market trends to help buyers make confident decisions.

Talha Shabbir

Business Development Manager

Talha Shabbir is the first conversation behind new relationships at SWK. He helps turn a broad property brief into a practical discussion about location, budget and timing, connecting clients with the right consultant and keeping communication clear from the outset.

Fareed Choudhary

Senior Property Consultant

Fareed Choudhary helps buyers look beyond a striking photograph. His approach centres on how a home will actually work: the layout, the neighbourhood, the ongoing costs and the questions to settle before making an offer. He supports clients through shortlisting, viewings and the next steps toward purchase.
