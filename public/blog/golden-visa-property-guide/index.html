Dubai property Golden Visa: questions to settle before you buy

18 May 2026 · 2 min read · Updated 24 October 2026

Dubai Land Department lists a property-investor route for a renewable ten-year residence permit with a qualifying purchase value of at least AED 2 million. That threshold is a starting point for an eligibility check, not a promise of approval for a particular purchase.

Start with the authority’s current requirements

Use the official DLD investor service page as the starting point. It describes the qualifying purchase value and documents for its route. Eligibility depends on your ownership and application circumstances. Before paying a reservation amount, ask the relevant service centre to confirm how the proposed transaction will be treated. Keep the answer with your purchase documents and check it again if the financing or ownership arrangement changes.

If there is a mortgage, ask a precise question

DLD’s service information specifies a bank letter showing AED 2 million paid for a mortgaged property. Do not assume that a property’s advertised value alone settles the mortgage question. Ask what evidence is required for your particular bank and purchase structure. A broker’s indicative mortgage calculation and an immigration eligibility assessment answer different questions; obtain both where relevant.

Choose a home that also works without the visa

Think through location, condition, running costs and how long you expect to own the property. Would it still suit your needs if the residency application took longer than expected? If it is intended as a rental investment, test the income assumptions separately. A property purchase creates financial commitments even while an application is being assessed. Leave room in your budget for both the property and the application process.

Prepare documents without sending them to strangers

Ask the official service channel for its current checklist and submit identity and ownership documents through that channel. Keep names and ownership details consistent across the documents you provide. Avoid sending passport scans or banking information through an open website chat. SWK’s website assistant can help you identify the next conversation; it does not assess documents or issue immigration decisions.

Separate official charges from service charges

Request a written breakdown that distinguishes government charges, medical or identity-related steps where applicable, and any professional assistance fee. Confirm the current amounts and what is included before instructing a provider. This guide does not quote a fixed all-in price or a guaranteed processing period, because those details need confirmation for the actual application.

Questions for your first conversation

Explain whether the property is already owned, whether there is financing and whose names appear on the ownership documents. Ask which documents establish qualifying value, what additional steps apply to family members and where the application should be submitted. Confirm the rules with the relevant authority before relying on the purchase for your residency plans. SWK can discuss the property search; the authority determines residency eligibility.

Official sources

  • DLD: Golden Visa application — Investor
  • GDRFA: Golden residence permit — Investors
